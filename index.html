01 / About me

Families, development, and wellbeing.

My work brings together developmental science, mental health, family research, prevention science, and digital health.

With academic training in cognitive science, neuroscience, and psychological medicine, I study adolescent mental health and the roles of parents and wider social and cultural environments in shaping wellbeing.

My experience spans quantitative research, scale validation, and intervention research. I am open to academic and research opportunities that advance work in mental health and human development.

Understanding wellbeing means looking at people in the family and social contexts where they grow.
