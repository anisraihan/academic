Understanding the mind, brain, and behaviour.

Hi, I'm Anis Raihan.

Cognitive Science · Neuroscience · Psychology

I am a psychology researcher with a focus on psychological medicine and child and adolescent health. My work examines how family processes, parenting, and evidence-based digital tools shape mental health and wellbeing.

I am particularly interested in understanding mental health across childhood, adolescence, and young adulthood through a biopsychosocial-spiritual perspective—recognising how biological, psychological, social, and spiritual dimensions intersect in shaping human development and wellbeing.

Through research and public communication, I aim to make psychological science meaningful beyond the journal, connecting evidence with the lives, relationships, and communities it seeks to understand.

Based in Malaysia · Open to research, postdoctoral, and lecturer opportunities
